Pricing questions

Why do you charge for the assessment?

Because it's real work: interviews, data review and a costed roadmap you can use with any vendor. It's fully credited toward a pilot if you go ahead with us within 60 days.

What does "final milestone only invoiced once the metric is met" mean?

Before a pilot starts we agree on one measurable success metric, such as calls answered, hours saved or documents processed without touch. The final milestone payment is due only when the pilot hits it.

What do software and AI usage cost on top?

It depends on the use case and volume. Every proposal lists expected monthly tool and model costs separately, and private deployments swap usage fees for a one-time hardware cost.

Can we cancel a managed plan?

Yes, with 30 days' notice. You keep everything we've built and all the documentation.
