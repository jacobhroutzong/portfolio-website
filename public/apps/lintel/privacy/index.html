Privacy policy

This policy explains what Lintel ("we") collects through this website and during installations, and how we use it.

What we collect on this website

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone, address or ZIP code, home details, Smart Home Scan answers, plan selections and messages.
  • Referral and campaign information: referral codes and campaign parameters attached to your visit.
  • Basic analytics: aggregate usage such as pages viewed, if analytics are enabled. We do not sell personal information.

Scan answers, savings estimates and plans are calculated in your browser and sent to us only if you submit a form.

Information about your home

During a visit or install we may record your floor plan (including LiDAR scans), device locations, network layout and photos of wiring. We use them to design, install and support your system, and we share them only with partners working on your project.

Accounts and passwords

Devices are registered to your accounts. We use temporary access during installation, store any credentials only in an encrypted password manager while the job is active, and remove our access at handoff unless you have a support plan and ask us to keep it.

Sharing

We share information with service providers that help us operate (hosting, email, scheduling, payments) and licensed partners (electricians, plumbers) working on your project, under contracts that limit their use of it, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at jacobroutzong@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws.

Changes

We'll post updates on this page and change the date above.
