The problem

Everyone sees the same homepage, the same bestsellers and the same email. Off-the-shelf "you may also like" widgets ignore your margins, inventory and customer lifetime value.

How we solve it

We model what customers buy together, what they're likely to buy next and which offers move them, then put those predictions into your storefront, email platform and ads, optimized for margin and inventory rather than clicks, and prove the impact with holdout tests.

What you get

  • Recommendation models (similar items, frequently bought together, next purchase)
  • Personalized email blocks and triggered flows
  • Offer and discount targeting rules that protect margin
  • A/B and holdout testing framework
  • Revenue-attribution dashboard

How it works

  1. Unify the data

    Bring order, product and behavior data into one clean customer view.

  2. Model and rank

    Train recommendation and next-purchase models with business rules for margin and stock.

  3. Deploy where customers are

    Storefront widgets, email (Klaviyo, Braze, HubSpot) and paid audiences.

  4. Prove it

    Run holdout tests so you know the incremental revenue, not just correlations.

Example (illustrative)

A store doing $250,000 a month online adds personalized recommendations and triggered emails. A 5% incremental lift, measured against a holdout group, is worth about $150,000 a year in additional revenue.

Questions we hear

How much traffic do we need?

A few thousand orders a year is usually enough for useful recommendations. Below that, curated rules and a good email program tend to beat a model.

How do we know it's working?

We keep a randomly selected holdout group that doesn't get personalization and report the revenue difference. No vanity metrics.

Will this replace our Shopify apps?

Sometimes it complements them. If an off-the-shelf app does the job, we'll say so; custom models earn their keep when margins, inventory or subscriptions make generic recommendations a poor fit.
