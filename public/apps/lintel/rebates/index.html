Rebates and discounts that help pay for upgrades

The federal 25C home-improvement credit ended for equipment installed after December 31, 2025. Utility rebates, demand-response payments and insurer discounts still help. We only list programs from official sources, with the date we last checked them.

Georgia Power smart thermostat rebate

50% of qualifying cost, up to $150. Georgia Power residential customers only; ENERGY STAR certification and a ducted central heating and air system required. Up to two smart thermostats per account per year. Confirm purchase-channel terms before buying. Source: 2026 program requirements, effective August 1, 2026.

What to ask

  • Your electric or gas utility: is there a rebate for an ENERGY STAR smart thermostat, and a demand-response credit for letting them adjust it on peak days?
  • Your home insurer: is there a discount for an automatic water shutoff valve or monitored leak sensors? Stand Insurance and Moen launched one in California and Florida in July 2026.
  • Your water district: is there a rebate for a WaterSense-labeled irrigation controller?
  • Your utility: are there EV charger rebates or off-peak charging rates, and do they require a specific charger?
  • Your insurer: is there a discount for monitored security or smart smoke and CO alarms?

The federal 25C energy-efficient home improvement credit does not apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2025 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, section 70505). This page is general information, not tax advice.
