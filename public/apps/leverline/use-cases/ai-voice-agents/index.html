The problem

Calls come in while your team is on a job, with a patient or after hours. Callers who reach voicemail often just call the next business on their list, and every missed call is a lost job you never see on your P&L.

How we solve it

A voice agent trained on your services, pricing rules, service area and FAQs picks up every call, books or reschedules appointments, captures details for quotes and transfers urgent calls to a person. Every call is transcribed and summarized in your CRM.

What you get

  • Voice agent configured with your scripts, services and escalation rules
  • Calendar and CRM or field-service integration (booking and rescheduling)
  • Missed-call text-back and after-hours coverage
  • Call recordings, transcripts and summaries
  • Weekly tuning from real call reviews

How it works

  1. Map your calls

    Review call logs and recordings to find the top reasons people call.

  2. Script and connect

    Write the conversation flows and connect booking, CRM and transfer numbers.

  3. Test real scenarios

    Run dozens of test calls, including edge cases and upset callers.

  4. Launch and tune

    Start with after-hours and overflow, then expand as results come in.

Example (illustrative)

An HVAC company misses about 40 calls a month. If the voice agent answers them and a quarter turn into booked jobs at a $650 average ticket, that's about $6,500 a month (roughly $78,000 a year) in jobs that used to go to competitors.

Questions we hear

Does it sound like a robot?

Current voice models sound natural and handle interruptions well. We still recommend the agent says it's an AI assistant. Callers mostly care that someone picked up and solved their problem.

What if a caller has an emergency or wants a human?

The agent recognizes urgent requests and transfers immediately to your on-call number, or takes a message and alerts you by text.

Can it book into ServiceTitan, Jobber or our calendar?

Yes. We integrate with common field-service, practice-management and calendar systems, or fall back to collecting details and creating a booking request.

How fast can we go live?

Typically one to three weeks, starting with after-hours and overflow calls so your team stays in control.
