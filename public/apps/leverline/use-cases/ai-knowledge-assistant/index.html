The problem

Knowledge lives in a dozen tools and a few senior people's heads. New hires take months to ramp, experts get interrupted all day, and the same question gets answered differently depending on who you ask.

How we solve it

We build a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) assistant over your approved sources. It respects existing permissions, cites every answer, says "I don't know" when the source isn't there, and improves as we review the questions it can't answer.

What you get

  • Connectors to SharePoint, Google Drive, Confluence, Notion, help desks or file shares
  • Permission-aware search with citations and source previews
  • Answer-quality evaluation set built from your real questions
  • Slack, Teams or web interface
  • Content-gap report showing what's missing from your documentation

How it works

  1. Pick the sources

    Inventory where knowledge lives and agree what the assistant can and can't read.

  2. Index and secure

    Documents are chunked, embedded and indexed with the original access controls attached.

  3. Evaluate

    Test against 50–200 real questions and tune retrieval until answers are accurate and cited.

  4. Roll out and improve

    Launch to one team, review unanswered questions weekly, then expand.

Example (illustrative)

A 120-person distributor's customer service reps each spend about five hours a week hunting through pricing rules, return policies and product specs. Cutting that in half for 30 reps at $38 an hour is worth about $148,000 a year, and every customer gets the same, correct answer.

Questions we hear

How do you stop it from making things up?

Answers are generated only from retrieved passages, every claim links to its source, and the assistant is instructed to decline when the sources don't cover the question. We measure accuracy on your own question set before launch.

Will people see documents they shouldn't?

No. Retrieval is filtered by the same permissions as the source system, so a user can only get answers from documents they could already open.

How is this different from Microsoft Copilot or Gemini?

Those tools work well inside their own suites. We focus on the sources they don't cover well, measurable accuracy on your questions, and the option to run everything privately.
