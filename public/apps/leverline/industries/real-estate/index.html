Questions from real estate & property management

Can AI create fair housing problems in marketing?

It can if nobody checks it. Housing ads on major platforms must use special ad categories that restrict targeting by age, gender and ZIP code, and AI-written copy needs review for steering language. We build those checks into the workflow.

What's the fastest AI win for a brokerage?

Speed to lead. Responding to new inquiries within minutes, around the clock, is usually the cheapest way to win more of the leads you already pay for.

Rules we design around

  • {'Fair Housing Act': 'no steering or discriminatory targeting'}
  • Housing special ad category rules on major ad platforms
  • TCPA consent for calls and texts
  • State licensing advertising rules

We're not a law firm. We build within these requirements and tell you when to involve counsel.
