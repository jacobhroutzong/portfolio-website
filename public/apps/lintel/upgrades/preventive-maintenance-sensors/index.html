The problem

Sump pumps fail during storms, freezers get left open, basements grow mold slowly, and HVAC filters clog without anyone noticing. Each one is cheap to catch and expensive to discover late.

What we do

We place sensors on the equipment that fails quietly, connect them to one hub and set thresholds that matter: pump cycling too often, temperature rising, humidity above 60%, smoke or CO alarms sounding while you're out.

What you'll notice

  • A warning before the basement floods
  • Freezer alerts
  • Healthier humidity

Questions homeowners ask

Do I need this if I already have leak sensors?

They cover different problems. Leak sensors catch water on the floor; these catch the equipment and conditions that lead to it.
