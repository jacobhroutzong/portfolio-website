Questions from accounting & bookkeeping firms

Is AI safe to use with client tax data?

It can be, if you control where the data goes. Tax preparers fall under the FTC Safeguards Rule and need a written information security plan; private or contractually protected deployments with access controls and logging fit within that program.

What should an accounting firm automate first?

Document intake and extraction is usually first. It touches every engagement, and the time savings are easy to measure during busy season.

Rules we design around

  • FTC Safeguards Rule (covers tax preparers)
  • IRS Publication 4557 and a written information security plan (WISP)
  • AICPA professional standards
  • State board of accountancy requirements

We're not a law firm. We build within these requirements and tell you when to involve counsel.
