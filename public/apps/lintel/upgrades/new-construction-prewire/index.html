The problem

Wiring is cheap with open walls and expensive after drywall. Builders' standard packages rarely include wired access points, cameras or speakers.

What we do

We review the plans, mark every drop with you, run and label cable during rough-in, and terminate it to a structured panel after drywall. You get a wiring map, and the house is ready for any system later.

What you'll notice

  • Wired Wi-Fi and cameras without patching walls later
  • A labeled wiring map
  • Higher-spec home
