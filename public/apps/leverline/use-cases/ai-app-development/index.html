The problem

AI demos are easy; AI products are hard. Teams struggle with unreliable outputs, unpredictable costs, latency and security, and many prototypes never reach customers.

How we solve it

We start with a working prototype in days to validate value with real users, then harden it: evaluation suites, guardrails, observability, caching and model routing for cost, and a clean handoff to your engineering team or ongoing support from ours.

What you get

  • Product discovery and AI feature design
  • Clickable prototype or live demo within one to two weeks
  • Production build (web, mobile or API) on your stack
  • Evaluation suite, guardrails and observability
  • Cost and latency optimization (caching, routing, smaller models)

How it works

  1. Frame the job

    Define the user, the job to be done and how success will be measured.

  2. Prototype fast

    Ship a working demo and put it in front of real users.

  3. Harden for production

    Add evaluations, guardrails, logging, security review and cost controls.

  4. Launch and iterate

    Release, watch real usage and improve on a regular cadence.

Questions we hear

How quickly can we see something working?

Usually within one to two weeks. We prototype first so you can test value with real users before committing to a full build.

Who owns the code?

You do. We build in your repositories with your conventions, and your team can take over at any point.

How do you control AI costs as usage grows?

Caching, routing simple requests to smaller models, batching and monitoring cost per user from day one, so margins hold as you scale.
