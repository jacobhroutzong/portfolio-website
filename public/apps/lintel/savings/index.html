About Leak Detection & Automatic Water Shutoff

How we count savings

We only count money you would actually keep as savings: lower energy bills, insurance discounts, avoided repair costs and rental turnover costs. Time saved is real, but it isn't cash, so we show it as hours and never fold it into payback.

Default numbers are starting assumptions you should replace with your own. For example, the leak-protection default uses a 1% yearly chance of a serious leak and a $30,000 claim, close to State Farm's average frozen-pipe claim from January 2024 to June 2025 (Carrier Management, January 2026). Smart thermostats default to 8% of heating and cooling costs, in line with ENERGY STAR's estimate as cited by the DC Sustainable Energy Utility. Change any number to match your home.
