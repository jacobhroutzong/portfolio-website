The problem

Hosts drive over to hand off keys, guests leave the AC at 64 degrees, a party draws neighbor complaints, and a leak in an empty unit runs for days.

What we do

We connect the lock and thermostat to your booking calendar so codes and temperatures follow each reservation, install a decibel-level noise monitor (no audio recording), and add leak and freeze protection with alerts to you and your cleaner.

What you'll notice

Questions homeowners ask

Are noise monitors allowed in rentals?

Decibel-level monitors that don't record audio are generally accepted by booking platforms when disclosed in the listing. Check your platform's current rules and local laws; we never install audio recording indoors.
