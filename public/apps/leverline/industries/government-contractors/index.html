Questions from defense & government contractors

Can we use AI with CUI?

Only with systems that meet your safeguarding obligations, and consumer AI tools generally don't. A model hosted inside your assessed environment, or fully air-gapped, keeps CUI within the boundary your system security plan describes.

What's the current CMMC timeline?

As of October 2026, Phase 1 requirements (in effect since November 10, 2025) remain in place, while the Phase 2 third-party assessment rollout was paused in July 2026 pending review. Requirements flow down to subcontractors, so plan as if certification is coming.

Rules we design around

  • CMMC 2.0 and NIST SP 800-171 for CUI
  • DFARS 252.204-7012 safeguarding and incident reporting
  • ITAR / EAR export controls
  • FedRAMP Moderate (or equivalent) for cloud services handling CUI

We're not a law firm. We build within these requirements and tell you when to involve counsel.
