Questions from healthcare practices & clinics

Is there HIPAA-compliant AI?

Tools aren't HIPAA compliant on their own. Your use of them is. That means a business associate agreement with any vendor that handles PHI, minimum-necessary access, audit logs and safeguards. Private deployments simplify this because PHI never leaves your environment.

Can an AI receptionist book into our scheduling system?

Usually, through the scheduling system's integration or API. Where there's no integration, the agent captures the request and your staff confirm it.

Rules we design around

  • HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules
  • Business associate agreements with every vendor that touches PHI
  • State health-privacy laws
  • Clinician review of any AI-generated patient content

We're not a law firm. We build within these requirements and tell you when to involve counsel.
