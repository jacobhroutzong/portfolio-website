Documents in, clean data out. No re-keying.

We automate the reading of invoices, purchase orders, bills of lading, contracts, claims and intake forms: extracting the fields you need, checking them against your records and pushing them into your systems.

Estimate your ROI Talk it through

The problem

Paper and PDFs still drive most back offices. Staff re-type the same fields into accounting, ERP or case-management systems, errors slip through, and processing backs up at month-end.

How we solve it

Vision-capable language models read documents the way a person does, including messy scans, tables and handwriting, and return structured data with a confidence score. Validation rules and matching catch mismatches, and only low-confidence items go to a human reviewer.

What you get

  • Extraction schemas for each document type
  • Validation against vendor lists, POs, price lists or case data
  • Human review queue for low-confidence fields
  • Integration with your accounting, ERP, DMS or case-management system
  • Accuracy and straight-through-processing dashboard

How it works

  1. Collect samples

    Gather a few hundred real documents per type, including the ugly ones.

  2. Define the schema

    Agree the fields, formats and validation rules with the people who use the data.

  3. Build and benchmark

    Measure field-level accuracy against hand-keyed data before anything goes live.

  4. Go live with review

    Start with human review on everything, then raise automation thresholds as accuracy proves out.

Example (illustrative)

A distributor processes 3,000 supplier invoices a month at about six minutes each. Automating 70% of the keying saves roughly 210 staff hours a month (about $88,000 a year at a $35 loaded cost) and shortens month-end close.

Questions we hear

How accurate is AI data extraction?

It depends on document quality and type, so we measure it on your documents before launch. Fields below a confidence threshold are routed to a reviewer, so the accuracy of what reaches your system is controlled rather than assumed.

Can it handle handwriting and poor scans?

Often, yes. Modern vision models handle far more than legacy OCR. Truly illegible items are flagged for review instead of guessed.

Can the documents stay on our servers?

Yes. Extraction can run on private models inside your network for confidential or regulated documents.
