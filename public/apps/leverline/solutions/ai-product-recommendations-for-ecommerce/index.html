AI Product Recommendations for E-commerce

Generic "bestsellers" widgets show every shopper the same products. Personalized recommendations use each customer's browsing and purchase history, plus your margins and stock levels, to show the products most likely to sell, on site and in email.

Estimate your ROI Book a call
What it looks like (illustrative)

A home-goods store with 20,000 SKUs adds complementary-product recommendations on product pages, a personalized "picked for you" email block and a replenishment flow for consumables. Recommendations exclude low-stock items and favor higher-margin alternatives when relevance is similar. A 10% holdout group never sees personalization, so the incremental revenue is measured rather than assumed.

How we deliver it

  1. Unify order, product, inventory and email engagement data

  2. Train co-purchase, similarity and next-purchase models

  3. Deploy to product pages, cart and email templates

  4. Measure lift against a persistent holdout group

What to plan for

  • Business rules for margin, stock and brand priorities matter as much as the model.
  • New shoppers get context-based recommendations until their history builds.
  • Personalization must follow your privacy notice and consent settings.

What we measure

  • Average order value
  • Repeat purchase rate
  • Revenue per email
  • Incremental revenue vs. holdout

Questions we hear

Will this work on Shopify?

Yes. We deliver recommendations through Shopify's storefront APIs or theme blocks and your email platform's dynamic content.

How big does our catalog need to be?

Personalization pays off most with a few hundred SKUs or more and repeat buyers. Smaller catalogs usually benefit more from curated bundles and email flows.

How much traffic do we need?

A few thousand orders a year is usually enough for useful recommendations. Below that, curated rules and a good email program tend to beat a model.

How do we know it's working?

We keep a randomly selected holdout group that doesn't get personalization and report the revenue difference. No vanity metrics.
