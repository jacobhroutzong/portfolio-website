Questions homeowners ask

How much does a smart home cost?

Our packages start at $1,140 for doorbell and locks and $1,430 for leak protection. A whole-home project is quoted after a design visit. The free scan gives you a price range first.

Do I have to buy the devices from you?

No. We can install devices you already own. Devices we supply come with our one-year workmanship warranty, and installs using your own devices are billed at a slightly higher labor rate.

Will it work with Alexa, Google or Apple?

Yes. We design around the ecosystem you already use and choose devices that support it, including Matter-certified devices that work across platforms.

What if something stops working later?

Every install includes a one-year workmanship warranty. Lintel Care members get updates, battery swaps, support and yearly check-ups.

Can you do electrical and plumbing work?

Line-voltage electrical work and main water-line plumbing are done by our licensed partners, billed through us, with permits when required.

Who handles permits for my project?

We review the scope and permit needs before you approve the work. The written plan identifies the licensed trade partner and who will arrange any required permits. Ask us about permit fees when reviewing your quote, since package estimates exclude them.

How long will installation take?

Packages usually take one installation day once the equipment and trade partners are ready. Custom work can take longer. We confirm the schedule after checking your home, including any equipment lead times, permits and inspections. Your plan includes setup and a walkthrough.
