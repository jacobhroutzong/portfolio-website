The problem

Adult children worry from a distance. Check-in calls go unanswered, falls often happen at night on the way to the bathroom, and nobody wants cameras in a parent's living room.

What we do

We install motion and door sensors that learn daily routines and alert chosen family members if, say, the kitchen hasn't been visited by 10am. Motion-activated path lighting runs from bedroom to bathroom, a voice assistant calls family hands-free, and a smart lock lets caregivers in without hiding a key. No cameras indoors unless the family chooses them.

What you'll notice

  • Fewer worried phone calls
  • A safer path at night
  • Caregivers let in without a hidden key

Questions homeowners ask

Does this replace a medical alert device?

No. It adds awareness of daily routines. For emergencies, keep a medical alert device or watch with fall detection; we can connect its alerts to the same family group.

How do you protect a parent's privacy?

Motion and door sensors don't record images or sound. The parent agrees to what's installed, and alerts go only to people they choose.
