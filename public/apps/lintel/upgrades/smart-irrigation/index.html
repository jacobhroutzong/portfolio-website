The problem

Old timers water on a fixed schedule through rainstorms and over-water shady zones while sunny ones brown out.

What we do

A weather-based controller adjusts watering from local forecasts and season. We set each zone's plant, soil and sun exposure and enroll you in rebates where your utility offers them.

What you'll notice

  • No watering in the rain
  • A healthier lawn
  • Lower outdoor water use
