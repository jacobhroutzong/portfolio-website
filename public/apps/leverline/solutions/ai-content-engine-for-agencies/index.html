AI Content Engine for Marketing Agencies

Clients expect more content, faster, for the same retainer. A content engine per client (trained on their voice, fed by interviews and existing assets and reviewed by your editors) lets an agency multiply output without multiplying hours, while keeping quality and brand consistency high.

Estimate your ROI Book a call
What it looks like (illustrative)

A 20-person agency managing social media for 30 clients sets up a voice profile and repurposing pipeline for each one. A monthly 30-minute client interview becomes a month of LinkedIn posts, short captioned videos, a newsletter and a blog article. Account managers review drafts in one queue, and production hours per client drop enough to take on more clients without hiring.

How we deliver it

  1. Build voice profiles and style rules for each client

  2. Set up transcription, repurposing and formatting pipelines

  3. Create an editorial approval queue for account managers

  4. Report output, engagement and hours per client

What to plan for

  • Client data and drafts stay separated in per-client workspaces.
  • Disclose AI assistance according to client contracts and platform rules.
  • White-label delivery keeps the client relationship with the agency.

What we measure

  • Assets per production hour
  • Gross margin per retainer
  • Client engagement metrics

Questions we hear

Can we resell this under our own brand?

Yes. Our agency partner program supports white-label delivery and revenue share.

Which platforms does it publish to?

LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X and blogs, through native schedulers or tools like Buffer and Hootsuite.

Won't AI content sound generic?

It will if it's prompted generically. We train the system on your own words, interviews and best posts, and a human editor approves everything. The ideas come from your experts; AI does the formatting and repurposing.

Will Google penalize AI-written content?

Google's spam policies target low-value content produced at scale to manipulate rankings, however it's made. Content grounded in real expertise and reviewed by people is fine. That's the only kind we make.
