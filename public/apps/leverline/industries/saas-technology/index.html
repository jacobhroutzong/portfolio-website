Questions from saas & technology companies

How do we keep AI feature costs under control?

Measure cost per active user from day one, cache aggressively, route easy requests to smaller models and set per-account limits. We design for margin, not just for the demo.

How soon can a churn model help?

Once you have a few hundred historical churn events and product usage data, a first model typically takes five to eight weeks including the save plays.

Rules we design around

  • SOC 2 commitments to your customers
  • Data processing agreements and sub-processor disclosures
  • EU AI Act transparency duties for EU users

We're not a law firm. We build within these requirements and tell you when to involve counsel.
