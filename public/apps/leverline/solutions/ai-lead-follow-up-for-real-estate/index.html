AI Lead Follow-Up for Real Estate Teams

Real estate teams pay heavily for leads, then lose many of them to slow first responses and inconsistent follow-up over long buying cycles. An AI lead engine responds within a minute, holds a natural conversation by text or email, qualifies timeline, budget and financing, and books appointments with the right agent.

Estimate your ROI Book a call
What it looks like (illustrative)

A 15-agent team receives several hundred portal and website leads a month. Each new lead gets a personalized text that references the property they viewed, a few qualifying questions and an offer to book a showing. Leads that aren't ready enter a nurture sequence with market updates and new listings matching their search, and the system alerts an agent the moment someone signals they're ready.

How we deliver it

  1. Connect lead sources and the team's CRM

  2. Write qualification flows and hand-off rules with top agents

  3. Configure nurture content tied to each lead's search criteria

  4. Track response time, appointments and closings by source

What to plan for

  • Texting requires proper consent under the TCPA, so consent capture is built into the flow.
  • Messages must avoid fair-housing steering language; we add review rules for that.
  • Agents stay in the loop. The AI books the appointment and the agent builds the relationship.

What we measure

  • Median response time
  • Lead-to-appointment rate
  • Closings from nurtured leads

Questions we hear

Will leads know they're texting with AI?

We recommend transparent disclosure. Most leads care that they got a fast, relevant answer and an easy way to reach an agent.

Which CRMs does it work with?

We integrate with common real estate CRMs and lead platforms that offer APIs or webhooks, and fall back to email routing where needed.

How much data do we need for predictive scoring?

A few hundred historical leads with known outcomes is enough to start. With less, we begin with a rules-based score built from your ideal-customer profile and switch to a model as data accumulates.

Will AI outreach hurt our brand?

Not the way we run it. AI drafts, your reps approve, volume is capped and deliverability is monitored. We optimize for replies from the right people, not raw send volume.
