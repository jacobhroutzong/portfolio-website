The problem

Apps list devices alphabetically, nobody knows which sensor is which, and a smart home designed without a floor plan puts nodes and sensors in the wrong places.

What we do

We scan each room with LiDAR, produce a measured floor plan, and plan Wi-Fi, sensors and devices on it. Then we build a live dashboard on a wall tablet and your phone that shows the house as a map, plus a printed plan for your records.

What you'll notice

  • The whole house at a glance
  • Faster troubleshooting
  • A floor plan you keep
