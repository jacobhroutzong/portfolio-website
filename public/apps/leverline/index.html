Questions we hear

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Mostly companies with 10 to 500 employees in professional services, healthcare practices, home services, e-commerce, distribution, SaaS and government contractors. If you have repetitive work, unanswered customers or underused data, there's usually a strong first use case.

How much does AI implementation cost?

Our free scan gives you an estimate. Fixed-fee assessments are $4,500, production pilots typically run $15k–$35k, and managed AI starts at $2,500/mo. Every proposal includes an ROI model so you can compare cost to value.

How fast will we see results?

Quick wins like an AI receptionist or a document workflow can go live in two to four weeks. Data-heavy projects such as forecasting or churn models usually take six to ten weeks.

Do we need to have our data in order first?

No. Many high-ROI use cases (voice agents, document processing, knowledge assistants) work with the data you have today. The scan tells you which opportunities fit your current readiness.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. We configure providers so your data isn't used for training, and for sensitive work we deploy private models where your data never leaves your environment.

What happens after the project ends?

You own everything we build. Most clients keep a managed plan so systems stay accurate and new automations keep shipping, but you can also hand it to your own team with full documentation.
