The problem

Most smart-home frustration is really a Wi-Fi problem. Devices drop off, cameras lag, and the router the internet company left in a closet can't reach the back bedroom, the garage or the yard.

What we do

We measure signal strength in every room, design the node layout, run Ethernet to a node when walls allow, and configure the network so smart devices live on their own segment, away from your laptops and phones. You get a coverage map before and after, so you can see the difference.

What you'll notice

  • No more dead zones
  • Video calls that don't freeze
  • Smart devices that stay connected

Questions homeowners ask

Do I need new Wi-Fi if my internet plan is fast?

Usually, yes. Your plan sets the speed coming into the house; your router sets how far it reaches inside. We test both, and if your current equipment is fine, we'll tell you.

Why put smart devices on a separate network?

Cheap smart devices are a common weak point. Keeping them on their own network segment means a compromised plug can't see your laptop or your files.

Will this work with my internet provider?

Yes. We work with any provider and leave their modem in place.
