We build AI that earns its keep.

Leverline exists because most businesses don't need more AI hype. They need someone to find the two or three places AI will actually pay off, build them properly and stick around to make sure they keep working.

How an engagement works

  1. Scan

    A free 10-minute scan scores your readiness and ranks your top opportunities with ROI estimates.

    Free · 10 minutes

  2. Assess

    Fixed fee · 2 weeks

    Monthly · cancel anytime

What we will and won't do

We will

  • Tell you when AI isn't the right answer
  • Agree on a success metric before we build
  • Keep sensitive data in your environment when it matters
  • Hand over code, prompts, models and documentation you own

We won't

  • Sell you a platform you'll be locked into
  • Use your data to train models for anyone else
  • Publish results we can't back with real numbers
  • Automate decisions that should stay with people

How we're different

  • ROI before code

    Every project starts with a baseline and a success metric. If the math doesn't work, we'll tell you and won't build it.

  • Private by default

    Sensitive data can stay on hardware or cloud accounts you control, with open-weight models and full audit logs.

  • Built on your stack

    We work inside the CRM, ERP, help desk and data platform you already run. You own the code, prompts and models.

  • Measured every month

    Live dashboards and a monthly value report show hours saved, revenue recovered and costs, not activity.
