Questions homeowners ask

Why might my price differ?

Older homes, plaster walls, three-story layouts and hard-to-reach attics take longer. The planner shows how those change the price.

Is the design visit required?

For packages, no; we can confirm details by video. For custom projects, yes. The $199 is credited to any project over $1,500 booked within 60 days.

Can I cancel Lintel Care?

Yes, anytime with 30 days' notice. Your devices keep working without it.
