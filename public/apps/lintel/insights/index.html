Use the research to plan your next step

Start with the question you need to answer: whether to hire an installer, what an upgrade could save, or how to keep control of your accounts and devices. Each article explains its sources so you can check what a claim measures and whether it applies to your household.

A national survey can describe how people use smart devices. It cannot tell you which product fits your wiring, plumbing or network. An insurance claim average describes past losses across a group of claims. It does not predict your home's risk or promise that a sensor will prevent every leak. Use those figures as context for questions.

Product support, prices and rebate programs can change after an article is published. Check the source date and confirm eligibility with your utility or insurer before including a discount in your budget. Our package prices are planning estimates until we confirm the details of your home and provide a written scope.

For a practical next step, browse the guides for a checklist, use the savings calculator with your own bills, or try the room planner to compare quantities. Bring the questions that remain to a design visit. Your daily routines, existing equipment and access conditions help determine which upgrades belong in the plan.
