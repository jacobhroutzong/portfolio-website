**Understand. Experiment. Prove.**

Empirava brings model development, interpretability, agent optimization, and application building into one research program. Each experiment records the question, setup, and checks that support its result.

## Preview and execution

[Open the protected Empirava preview](https://empirava.jacobroutzong.dev/)

The hosted preview requires authorized access and presents saved research material in read-only form. The owner runs training, evaluation, interventions, and agent or application experiments locally with the required models, data, and compute.

## Research and engineering

### Model development and evaluation

The workflow started in Tunera. It tracks training inputs, run configuration, and evaluation separately so saved runs can be compared against the same question. Evaluation uses task-specific checks and controls to document what changed and which outcomes were measured. [Tunera workflow notes](/projects/tunera/)

### Causarium interpretability research

Activation swaps in Pythia-70M and Qwen3.5-2B test how model internals affect predictions. The exhibit records logit margins, donor controls, and identity shams, with pinned model revisions and source digests for the released evidence. [Causarium research exhibit](/projects/causarium/) · [Causarium research console](/projects/causarium/lab/) · [Causarium evidence JSON](/media/causarium/evidence-v1.json)

### Harness² optimization

Harness² studies changes to the prompts, rules, memory, and tools around a frozen model. The lab shows how candidate changes are proposed, probed, and composed using execution traces. Its default simulator illustrates that process. Model-backed performance requires separate measurements. [Harness² Lab](/projects/harness2-lab/) · [Harness² source](https://github.com/jacobhroutzong/harness2-lab)

### Recursa application building

Recursa studies how models inspect specifications and produce applications. The recorded comparisons track model calls, builds, and browser checks. The case study documents the limited first-pass results and identifies repairs or host assembly in the showcases. [Recursa research case study](/projects/recursa/) · [Recursa application replay](/projects/recursa/studio/) · [Recursa source](https://github.com/jacobhroutzong/recursa)

## Concept Atlas and competency evidence

The Concept Atlas connects concepts to the experiments that illustrate them. Competency evidence links engineering skills to work a reviewer can inspect, such as an evaluation design, a recorded intervention, a benchmark trace, or a verified browser workflow. The linked research records describe the measured outcomes and their limitations.
