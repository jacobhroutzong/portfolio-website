The problem

Water damage is one of the most expensive things that happens to a house. State Farm reported its average frozen-pipe claim payment was more than $30,000 between January 2024 and June 2025, and most leaks start somewhere nobody is looking: under a sink, behind a washer or in a basement.

What we do

A flow-monitoring shutoff valve learns your normal water use and closes the main line when it sees a burst, a running toilet or a slow leak. Sensors under sinks, the water heater, washer and dishwasher catch drips early. Freeze sensors warn you before a pipe bursts. Our partner plumber installs the valve; we set up the sensors and alerts.

Questions homeowners ask

Will my insurance company give me a discount?

Some do. Programs vary by insurer and state, so call your agent before you buy. Stand Insurance and Moen launched a program in California and Florida in July 2026 that discounts premiums for homes with an active automatic shutoff. We provide the installation documentation insurers ask for.

What happens if the valve shuts off while I'm in the shower?

It learns your normal water use, so showers, irrigation and laundry don't trigger it. If it ever closes by mistake, you reopen it from the app or with the manual lever.

Why does a plumber install the valve?

It goes on your main water line. Most areas require licensed plumbing work for that, so our partner plumber installs it and we handle everything else.
