Questions from home services & contractors

How much is a missed call worth to a home service business?

Multiply missed calls by your booking rate and average ticket. For many HVAC and plumbing companies a few dozen missed calls a month adds up to tens of thousands of dollars a year. Our ROI calculator does the math with your numbers.

Will an AI receptionist work with ServiceTitan or Jobber?

Yes. We connect to common field-service platforms to check availability and create bookings, or capture details for your dispatcher where an integration isn't available.

Rules we design around

  • TCPA consent for calls and texts
  • Call-recording consent laws (vary by state)
  • Accurate pricing and licensing claims

We're not a law firm. We build within these requirements and tell you when to involve counsel.
