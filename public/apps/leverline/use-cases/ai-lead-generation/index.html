The problem

Most leads get the same slow, generic follow-up. Reps chase whoever shouted last, good prospects go cold, and nobody can say which marketing channels produce revenue rather than form fills.

How we solve it

We train a scoring model on your closed-won and closed-lost history, enrich leads with firmographic and intent data, and trigger instant, personalized responses for high-fit leads. Outbound prospecting uses the same profile of your best customers to find look-alikes.

What you get

  • Ideal-customer profile built from your CRM data
  • Enrichment plus predictive fit and intent scoring
  • Speed-to-lead automation (email, SMS or a call task within minutes)
  • AI-drafted, rep-approved personalized outreach
  • Source-to-revenue attribution dashboard

How it works

  1. Learn from your wins

    Analyze closed deals to find the traits and behaviors that predict a sale.

  2. Score and route

    Score every lead as it arrives and route hot leads to the right rep instantly.

  3. Personalize follow-up

    Draft tailored first touches and sequences your team reviews and sends.

  4. Close the loop

    Feed outcomes back into the model monthly so scores keep improving.

Example (illustrative)

A B2B services firm gets 200 inbound leads a month and closes 4% at an average $9,000 deal. If instant follow-up and better prioritization lift the close rate by a relative 20%, that's about 1.6 extra deals a month, or roughly $173,000 a year in new revenue.

Questions we hear

How much data do we need for predictive scoring?

A few hundred historical leads with known outcomes is enough to start. With less, we begin with a rules-based score built from your ideal-customer profile and switch to a model as data accumulates.

Will AI outreach hurt our brand?

Not the way we run it. AI drafts, your reps approve, volume is capped and deliverability is monitored. We optimize for replies from the right people, not raw send volume.

Does this work with our CRM?

We work with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and most CRMs with an API. Scores and next actions show up where reps already work.
