Get production AI out of the data platform you already pay for.

If your data lives in Databricks or Snowflake, the fastest path to AI usually runs through it. We build governed ML models, AI functions and agents directly on your platform, plus the MLOps to keep them healthy.

The problem

Many companies have a modern data platform but few models in production. Data science stays stuck in notebooks, business teams can't self-serve, and nobody owns monitoring once a model ships.

How we solve it

We work inside your platform's native tooling, such as Snowflake Cortex (AI SQL functions, Cortex Analyst, Cortex Search, Cortex Agents) or Databricks (MLflow, Model Serving, Agent Bricks, AI/BI Genie), to ship models and AI features with governance, monitoring and cost controls built in from day one.

What you get

  • Use-case prioritization and feasibility review on your data
  • Feature pipelines and governed training datasets
  • Models deployed with MLflow or the Snowflake model registry and serving
  • Semantic models and metric views for natural-language analytics
  • Monitoring for drift, data quality and spend
  • Handover documentation and team enablement

How it works

  1. Platform review

    Assess data quality, governance (Unity Catalog or Horizon), costs and team skills.

  2. Pick the first model

    Choose a use case with clear value and data you already trust.

  3. Build in-platform

    Develop features, train, register and serve the model where your data lives.

  4. Operate

    Add monitoring, retraining schedules, cost alerts and a runbook your team owns.

Example (illustrative)

A mid-market retailer already on Snowflake wants faster answers from its data. A Cortex-based analytics assistant over a well-defined semantic view lets 25 managers answer routine questions themselves, saving about three hours a week each at $60, or roughly $234,000 a year of analyst and manager time.

Questions we hear

Should we use Snowflake Cortex or build our own models?

Use native AI functions where they're good enough. They're governed and quick to ship. Custom models make sense when accuracy, cost at scale or specialized data demand it. We test both on your use case.

Do you work with Databricks and Snowflake on the same project?

Yes. Many companies run both, and we design pipelines and governance that don't fight each other.

Will our team be able to maintain it?

That's the goal. We build with your conventions, document everything and pair with your engineers so they own it after handover.
