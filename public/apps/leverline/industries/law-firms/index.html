Questions from law firms

Can law firms use generative AI ethically?

Yes, with care. ABA Formal Opinion 512 says lawyers using generative AI must understand the tools, protect client confidences, supervise the output and bill fairly. A private deployment plus a written firm policy addresses most of those obligations directly.

Which AI use cases pay off fastest for a law firm?

Usually intake (never missing a potential client), first-draft generation and document summarization. They're quick to deploy and save time on work clients increasingly resist paying for.

Rules we design around

  • ABA Model Rules 1.1 (competence) and 1.6 (confidentiality)
  • ABA Formal Opinion 512 on generative AI tools (2024)
  • State bar AI guidance
  • Client engagement and outside-counsel terms
