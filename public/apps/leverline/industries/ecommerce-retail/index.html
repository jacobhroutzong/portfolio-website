Questions from e-commerce & retail

What's the best first AI project for an online store?

For most stores, support automation or personalized email flows. Both pay back quickly and use data you already have. Forecasting becomes the priority once inventory is a large share of working capital.

Do we need a data warehouse first?

Not to start. Your store platform, email tool and help desk hold enough data for early wins. A warehouse becomes worthwhile as you add channels and models.

Rules we design around

  • State consumer privacy laws (such as CCPA/CPRA)
  • Email and SMS marketing consent
  • FTC rules on reviews and endorsements
  • Accessible storefront content

We're not a law firm. We build within these requirements and tell you when to involve counsel.
