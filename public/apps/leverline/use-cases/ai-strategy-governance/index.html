The problem

Leadership knows AI matters but can't separate hype from value. Employees use unapproved tools, nobody owns the risk, and investments get made one vendor demo at a time.

How we solve it

A short, structured assessment produces a prioritized roadmap with ROI estimates, a practical acceptable-use policy, a lightweight governance process aligned with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO/IEC 42001, and training so people actually use the approved tools well.

What you get

  • Prioritized use-case roadmap with ROI and effort estimates
  • Acceptable-use policy and approved-tool list

Questions we hear

We're small. Do we need AI governance?

You need the right-sized version. For most small businesses that's a one-page usage policy, an approved-tool list and a simple review step for anything touching customer data. It takes days, not months.

Which AI regulations apply to us?

It depends on where you operate and how you use AI. As of late 2026 the EU AI Act's high-risk obligations are scheduled for December 2027 for most standalone systems, and US state laws such as Colorado's have been revised and delayed. We map what applies to your use cases. We're not a law firm, so we'll tell you when to involve counsel.
