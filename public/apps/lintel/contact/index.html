What to include in your message

Start with your ZIP code and the problem you want to solve. Tell us whether you own the home, are moving in, or are planning a renovation. Mention rooms with weak Wi-Fi, devices that disconnect, or routines you would like to simplify. You do not need to choose a package first.

If you already own devices, include their brands and model names and the app you use to control them. Tell us about access restrictions, older wiring or upcoming building work. We check compatibility and access before confirming the scope. Keep passwords, entry codes and detailed floor plans out of this first message.

Include your preferred timing and a comfortable budget range if you have one. We can help you decide which work to do first and which upgrades can wait. Sending an inquiry does not book an installation. We confirm the scope, price and schedule with you before work begins.

What happens next

  1. We review your noteUsually the same day.
  2. Design visit60–90 minutes at your home. $199, credited to your project.
  3. Fixed-price planA written plan and price, usually within two days.
