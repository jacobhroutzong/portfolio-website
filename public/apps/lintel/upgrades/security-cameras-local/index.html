The problem

Battery cameras miss events, cloud plans cost money every month, and footage of your family ends up on a company's servers. Bad placement catches the top of a hat and misses the face.

What we do

We plan camera angles that capture faces and plates, run cable to each camera, and record locally. You view everything in one app, with alerts only for people and cars. Matter 1.5 added cameras to the standard, and we choose equipment that fits the system you already use.

Questions homeowners ask

Is it legal to record my yard?

Recording video of your own property is generally allowed. Audio recording rules are stricter and vary by state, so we leave audio off unless you choose otherwise, and we mask neighbors' windows.

What if someone steals the recorder?

We put it somewhere out of sight and can add an encrypted off-site backup of event clips if you want one.
