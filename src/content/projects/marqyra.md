[Open Marqyra (owner-only)](https://marqyra.jacobroutzong.dev/). The workspace requires the enrolled owner's Google account.

## Preserve the strategy behind each campaign

Product positioning and channel choices change over time. Marqyra saves each strategy revision and links a campaign brief to the version that informed it. A brief records the goal, message, planned budget, and review status. Tracked links add UTM parameters so reports can identify the campaign.

## Keep reported results traceable

Results can be entered by hand or imported from CSV. A preview identifies duplicates and conflicting rows before they are saved. Each result retains its source, reporting period, and attribution basis. Corrections record a reason and retain earlier versions, so a lesson can still point to the evidence that supported it at the time.

## Scope of the release

This release covers campaign planning, manual measurement, and evidence-linked lessons. AI assistance, advertising and analytics integrations, and enterprise collaboration are outside the current release.
