The problem

Ticket volume grows faster than headcount. Customers wait hours for answers to simple questions, agents burn out on repetitive tickets, and the old scripted chatbot frustrates everyone.

How we solve it

An AI agent grounded in your help content and connected (read-only at first) to orders, bookings or accounts resolves routine requests end to end. Every other ticket is tagged, prioritized and given an AI-drafted reply your agents can edit and send.

What you get

  • AI agent for web chat, email and messaging channels
  • Grounding in your help center, policies and macros
  • Secure lookups (order status, appointments, account info)
  • Triage, tagging, sentiment and priority routing
  • Agent-assist drafts and conversation summaries

How it works

  1. Analyze tickets

    Cluster 6–12 months of tickets to find what's automatable and what's not.

  2. Ground the agent

    Connect knowledge and systems; write escalation rules and tone guidelines.

  3. Shadow mode

    The AI drafts while agents decide, so accuracy is measured safely.

  4. Go live in stages

    Turn on automation intent by intent as each one proves itself.

Example (illustrative)

A subscription business handles 4,000 tickets a month at about $6 each. Resolving 35% automatically saves roughly $100,000 a year and cuts first response time from hours to seconds for those customers.

Questions we hear

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. We recommend clear disclosure, and in some places it's required. Customers care most about a fast, correct answer and an easy path to a person.

What happens when the AI doesn't know?

It says so and hands the conversation to a person with a summary, so the customer never has to repeat themselves.

Can it take actions like refunds or cancellations?

It can, within limits you set, for example refunds under a threshold, with everything logged. Most clients start read-only and add actions once accuracy is proven.
