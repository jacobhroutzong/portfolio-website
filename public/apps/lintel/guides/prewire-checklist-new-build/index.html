The Pre-Wire Checklist for a New Build

The cheapest wire you'll ever run is the one installed before drywall. This checklist covers what to mark on your plans so the house is ready for whatever technology you add, now or in ten years.

Illustrative scenario

A family building a 2,800 sq ft two-story marks 16 drops at framing: two ceiling access points per floor, four camera locations under the eaves, TV locations in three rooms, the office, a doorbell, and conduit to the attic. We run and label the cable during rough-in and terminate it to a structured panel in the utility room after drywall.

Checklist

  • Ceiling access points, one per 800 to 1,200 sq ft per floor as a starting rule
  • Camera drops at every exterior door and the driveway
  • Two cable runs to each TV location and the office
  • Doorbell wire and a transformer at the front door
  • Speaker wire to the kitchen, living room and patio
  • Shade power or blocking at large windows
  • A conduit from the panel to the attic for future runs

Worth knowing

  • Coordinate rough-in with the electrician so boxes and keypads line up.
  • Photograph every wall before drywall; we include the photos in your wiring map.
  • Plan the structured panel near power and ventilation.

Questions homeowners ask

Is wireless good enough now?

Wireless devices are great, but the access points and cameras that carry them work best wired. Pre-wiring keeps your options open.
