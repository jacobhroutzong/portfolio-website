Stop paying for clicks that never buy.

Ad platforms optimize toward whatever signal you send them. We connect your first-party sales data so Google, Meta and LinkedIn bid for customers who buy and stay, rather than people who only fill out forms.

Estimate your ROI Talk it through

The problem

Platforms optimize for cheap leads, privacy changes have cut tracking, and creative testing is slow. The result is rising acquisition costs and reports that don't match your revenue.

How we solve it

We build server-side conversion tracking, send qualified-lead and revenue events back to each platform, score customers by predicted value to seed look-alike audiences and value-based bidding, and use AI to generate and test creative variations at a pace a small team can't.

What you get

  • Server-side tracking and conversion APIs (Meta CAPI, Google enhanced & offline conversions, LinkedIn CAPI)
  • Predicted-value customer scores for audiences and bidding
  • AI creative variation pipeline with brand guardrails
  • Budget pacing and anomaly alerts
  • Revenue-based reporting across channels

How it works

  1. Audit the signal

    Check what each platform currently learns from and where conversions are lost.

  2. Fix the data loop

    Send qualified and revenue events back server-side, matched to campaigns.

  3. Model customer value

    Score customers by predicted value and seed audiences and bidding with it.

  4. Test creative faster

    Generate on-brand variations and let performance data pick the winners.

Example (illustrative)

An e-commerce brand spending $40,000 a month on ads switches to optimizing for revenue-weighted conversions instead of all purchases. A 12% efficiency gain on that spend is worth roughly $58,000 a year, before any lift from better creative.

Questions we hear

Is this compliant with privacy rules?

Conversion APIs send hashed identifiers under your consent settings, and we document what's shared with each platform. Some categories (housing, employment and credit) have special ad rules that limit targeting, and we design within them.

How much ad spend do we need for this to matter?

Generally a few thousand dollars a month per platform. Below that, fixing tracking and creative usually pays off faster than modeling.

Do you run our campaigns too?

We focus on the data and AI layer and work alongside your in-house team or agency. If you need campaign management, we'll bring in a partner.
