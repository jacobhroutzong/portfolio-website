Families Helping a Parent Age in Place

Peace of mind for you, independence for them.

Most people want to stay in their own homes as they get older, and their families want to know they're all right. A few well-chosen sensors and a voice assistant can give both, without cameras or complicated apps.

Questions homeowners ask

Will my parent need to use an app?

No. Most of it runs on its own, and voice and wall switches handle the rest. Family members use the app.

Who pays and who owns the accounts?

The family member who manages the home usually owns the accounts, with the parent's agreement. We set up shared access so more than one person can help.
