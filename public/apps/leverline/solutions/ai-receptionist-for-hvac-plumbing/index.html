AI Receptionist for HVAC, Plumbing & Home Services

When a furnace quits on a January night or a pipe bursts on a Sunday, the homeowner keeps calling until someone answers. An AI receptionist makes sure that's you: it picks up every call, books the job into your field-service software and gets emergencies to your on-call tech in seconds.

Estimate your ROI Book a call
What it looks like (illustrative)

A 12-truck HVAC and plumbing company routes overflow and after-hours calls to a voice agent. It confirms the address is in the service area, identifies the issue, offers open windows from the dispatch board, explains the diagnostic fee and books the job. No-heat and active-leak calls trigger an immediate transfer and a text to the on-call tech, and every call summary lands in the CRM.

How we deliver it

  1. Pull call logs to measure missed calls by hour and day

  2. Script booking, pricing-policy and emergency flows

  3. Connect ServiceTitan, Jobber or Housecall Pro plus your phone system

  4. Launch after-hours first, then overflow during peak season

What to plan for

  • Emergency detection and transfer rules matter more than anything else, so we test them hardest.
  • Membership and maintenance-plan customers can get priority routing.
  • Missed-call text-back recovers callers who hang up before the agent answers.

What we measure

  • Missed-call rate
  • Jobs booked from after-hours calls
  • Average ticket of AI-booked jobs

Questions we hear

Can it quote prices?

It can share whatever pricing you approve (diagnostic fees, service-call minimums, financing options) and avoids quoting jobs that need a technician's assessment.

How does it handle angry customers?

It stays calm, acknowledges the issue and transfers to a person when the caller asks or the situation calls for it. We test those scenarios before launch.

Does it sound like a robot?

Current voice models sound natural and handle interruptions well. We still recommend the agent says it's an AI assistant. Callers mostly care that someone picked up and solved their problem.

What if a caller has an emergency or wants a human?

The agent recognizes urgent requests and transfers immediately to your on-call number, or takes a message and alerts you by text.
