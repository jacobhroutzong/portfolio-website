The problem

Consistent content takes hours every week, agencies are expensive, and generic AI posts sound like everyone else's. Most businesses post in bursts, then go quiet for months.

How we solve it

We capture your voice from your best existing content, set up a pipeline that repurposes one source (a 20-minute interview, a webinar, a case study) into dozens of formatted assets, schedule them, and feed performance back so the system learns what your audience responds to.

What you get

  • Brand voice guide and style rules the models follow
  • Repurposing pipeline for long-form audio and video
  • Short-form clip selection, captions and thumbnails
  • Approval workflow and publishing schedule
  • Monthly performance review and topic plan

How it works

  1. Capture your voice

    Analyze your best-performing content and interview your experts.

  2. Build the pipeline

    Set up transcription, repurposing, formatting and scheduling.

  3. Human approval

    An editor reviews every asset; nothing ships unapproved.

  4. Learn and iterate

    Double down on the topics and formats that drive engagement and leads.

Questions we hear

Won't AI content sound generic?

It will if it's prompted generically. We train the system on your own words, interviews and best posts, and a human editor approves everything. The ideas come from your experts; AI does the formatting and repurposing.

Will Google penalize AI-written content?

Google's spam policies target low-value content produced at scale to manipulate rankings, however it's made. Content grounded in real expertise and reviewed by people is fine. That's the only kind we make.

Do you handle short-form video?

Yes. We clip long recordings into short vertical videos with captions and hooks for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
