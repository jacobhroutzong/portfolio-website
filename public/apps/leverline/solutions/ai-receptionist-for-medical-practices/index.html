AI Receptionist for Medical & Dental Practices

Front desks juggle check-ins, insurance questions and a phone that never stops ringing. When patients can't get through, they book elsewhere or don't book at all. An AI receptionist answers every call, handles scheduling and routine questions, and hands clinical or complex calls to staff with a summary.

Estimate your ROI Book a call
What it looks like (illustrative)

A three-location dental group sends overflow and after-hours calls to a voice agent. It books new-patient exams and hygiene visits into the practice-management schedule, reschedules appointments, answers questions about hours, locations and accepted insurance, and sends confirmations. Calls that mention pain, swelling or an emergency are transferred immediately to the on-call line.

How we deliver it

  1. Review call recordings to rank the top reasons patients call

  2. Configure scheduling rules, scripts and escalation triggers

  3. Integrate with the practice-management system under a BAA

  4. Launch on after-hours and overflow, then expand

What to plan for

  • Any voice vendor that hears or stores PHI must sign a business associate agreement.
  • The agent never gives clinical advice; symptom-related calls go to staff immediately.
  • Call-recording consent rules vary by state, so greetings are configured to match.

What we measure

  • Call answer rate
  • New-patient appointments booked
  • No-show rate
  • Front-desk time on phones

Questions we hear

Can an AI receptionist verify insurance?

It can collect insurance details and, where your systems support it, trigger an eligibility check. Complex benefit questions go to staff.

Will older patients be comfortable with it?

The agent speaks naturally, lets callers interrupt and offers a person at any time. Most callers simply want to be helped without waiting on hold.

Does it sound like a robot?

Current voice models sound natural and handle interruptions well. We still recommend the agent says it's an AI assistant. Callers mostly care that someone picked up and solved their problem.

What if a caller has an emergency or wants a human?

The agent recognizes urgent requests and transfers immediately to your on-call number, or takes a message and alerts you by text.
