New Homeowners

Moving in is the best time to make the house smart.

The weeks after closing are when you find the dead Wi-Fi zones, wonder who still has keys and learn where the water heater is. It's also the easiest time to fix all of it, before furniture blocks the walls and routines set in.

Questions homeowners ask

What should I do first in a new house?

Secure the doors, find and protect the water main, and make sure the Wi-Fi reaches where you'll work and sleep. Everything else can follow.

Can I reuse the previous owner's smart devices?

Often, yes, once they're factory reset and moved to your accounts. We check each one and tell you what's worth keeping.
