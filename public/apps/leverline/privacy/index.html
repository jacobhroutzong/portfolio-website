Privacy policy

Template. Have a qualified attorney review and adapt this before launch.

This policy explains what Leverline ("we") collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit a form, request an estimate, complete the AI Opportunity Scan or join the referral program, we collect what you enter: typically your name, email, company, role, industry, team size, budget range, scan answers and message.
  • Referral and campaign information. If you arrive through a referral link or a tagged campaign link, we store the referral code and campaign parameters with your submission so we can credit the right partner.
  • Basic analytics. If analytics are enabled, we collect aggregate usage data such as pages viewed and referring sites. We do not sell personal information.

Scan answers and ROI calculations are processed in your browser. They are only sent to us if you submit a form.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, prepare estimates and proposals, and deliver services you ask for
  • To credit referral partners and pay commissions
  • To improve our website and services
  • To send occasional updates if you opt in (you can unsubscribe at any time)

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate (for example, hosting, email and CRM providers) under contracts that limit their use of it, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep inquiry and client records for as long as needed to provide services and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing jacobroutzong@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws.

Client data in AI projects

Data we process during client engagements is governed by the contract for that engagement, not this website policy. We do not use client data to train models for anyone else.

Changes

We'll post updates on this page and change the date above.
