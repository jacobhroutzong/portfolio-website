The problem

Smart bulbs stop working when someone flips the switch, porch lights stay on all day, and coming home to a dark house happens every winter.

What we do

Our licensed electrician partner replaces switches with smart dimmers (no neutral wire needed for many models), and we program scenes, schedules and keypads. Lights still work from the wall, and they also respond to sunset, motion, voice and your schedule.

Questions homeowners ask

Why not just use smart bulbs?

Bulbs go offline when someone turns off the switch. Smart switches keep the wall working for everyone and control any bulb.

Do I need an electrician for smart switches?

Switches are line-voltage work, and most states require a licensed electrician for paid electrical work. Our partner electrician does that part, and we handle programming.
