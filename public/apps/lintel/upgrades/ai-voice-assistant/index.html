The problem

Voice assistants misunderstand device names, half the house isn't connected, and many people don't want recordings of their family sent to the cloud.

What we do

The new AI assistants understand context, so "turn off the lights" from the kitchen means the kitchen. We set up Alexa+, Gemini for Home or Apple's Siri with clean room and device names. For a privacy-first home, we install Home Assistant with local voice satellites. Its August 2026 release added built-in llama.cpp and LiteLLM integrations, so device commands can run entirely in the house.

Questions homeowners ask

Can voice control work without the internet?

Yes, for device control. A local Home Assistant setup handles lights, locks and climate in the house. General questions and music still need a cloud service.

Which assistant should I pick?

It depends on your phones, your privacy preference and what you already own. The Smart Home Scan recommends one, and we can mix them.
